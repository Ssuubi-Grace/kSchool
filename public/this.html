Glad to have you here

Welcome to Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School. A private elementary school licensed and registered by the Ministry of Education and Sports. Located in Gitta village, Kasangati Town Council, Wakiso district, 5Km off Gayaza-Zirobwe Road, the school stands as a beacon of learning offering a warm and welcoming environment. Our dedicated team of teaching and non-teaching staff ensures a nurturing and conducive atmosphere for any child's growth. At Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School, we go beyond academics, providing a rich tapestry of activities encompassing games, sports, life skills, and talent grooming.

Choose Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School, where every child is given the wings to soar. Join us on the path where quality education begins. Watch your child blossom into a confident and capable individual. Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School, where education meets excellence without regret.

Meet the Visionary: Mrs. Margaret N. Nakiwala

Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School is a reflection of one woman's unwavering dedication and vision: Mrs. Margaret N. Nakiwala. With a foundation built on love and a commitment to community, Mrs. Nakiwala has cultivated an environment where children are nurtured to become their best selves. Through her tireless efforts, she has made a lasting impact on countless young lives, empowering them to reach for their dreams and achieve their full potential.
