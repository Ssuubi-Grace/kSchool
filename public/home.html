39 YEARS OF PROVIDING QUALITY EDUCATION

Welcome to Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School. A private elementary school licensed and registered by the Ministry of Education and Sports. Located in Gitta village, Kasangati Town Council, Wakiso district, 5Km off Gayaza-Zirobwe Road, the school stands as a beacon of learning offering a warm and welcoming environment. Our dedicated team of teaching and non-teaching staff ensures a nurturing and conducive atmosphere for any child's growth. At Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School, we go beyond academics, providing a rich tapestry of activities encompassing games, sports, life skills, and talent grooming. Choose Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School, where every child is given the wings to soar. Join us on the path where quality education begins. Watch your child blossom into a confident and capable individual. Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School, where education meets excellence without regret.

A Message from Our Founder & Director

FOUNDER'S MESSAGE

I'm Sarah Muwonge, the founder and director of Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School. My passion for education has always driven me, and I believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn and thrive. When I moved to this village after marriage, I realized the lack of educational opportunities for the children here. With the unwavering support of my husband, we decided to take a leap of faith. I left my job in the city, and together, we embarked on a mission to establish Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School, the first school in this community.

DIRECTOR'S MESSAGE

As the director, I am dedicated to ensuring Kutya Mukama Nursery and Primary School provides a nurturing environment, quality education, and essential skills for our students. Witnessing their growth and potential blossom brings immense joy and motivates us to keep striving for excellence. We believe education is the key to unlocking a brighter future for our children and the community as a whole.

WHAT WE OFFER

Quality Education

At our school, we are committed to providing every student with a high-quality education that ignites their curiosity, fosters their talents, and empowers them to reach their full potential. Our classrooms are vibrant spaces where dedicated teachers guide students through a rigorous academic curriculum that encompasses essential skills, knowledge, and values. We believe that every child deserves access to a world-class education, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Games and Sports

Our school recognizes the importance of physical activity and sports in a child's development. We offer a wide range of sports and recreational activities to promote physical fitness, teamwork, and sportsmanship. Whether it's on the soccer field, in the basketball court, or during a friendly game of tag, our students learn valuable life skills that extend beyond the classroom.

Talent Grooming

We believe that every child possesses unique talents waiting to be discovered and nurtured. Our school offers a variety of programs and activities designed to identify and develop these talents. From music and arts to science and technology, we provide opportunities for students to explore their interests and hone their skills, ensuring they grow into well-rounded individuals ready to make a positive impact on the world.

Life Skills Development

In addition to academic excellence, we prioritize the development of essential life skills that prepare our students for success in an ever-changing world. Our comprehensive life skills programs teach practical abilities such as critical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and emotional intelligence. We equip our students with the tools they need to navigate life's challenges and seize its opportunities with confidence and resilience.

Conducive Environment

Our school campus provides a safe, inclusive, and nurturing environment where every student feels valued and respected. We have created a welcoming atmosphere that fosters a sense of belonging and encourages positive interactions among students, teachers, and staff. Our commitment to maintaining a conducive learning environment ensures that every child can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.
